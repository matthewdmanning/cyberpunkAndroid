## 2. Mechanical, Electronic & Bio-Digital Operating Principles

### Autonomous Arc-Weld Cutting Cycle
- **Ballistic Casing Penetration:** Armored corporate conduits utilize outer layers of hardened boron carbide and steel braiding to defeat conventional taps. The HV-480 clamps around the pipe with high-pressure hydraulic jaws, sealing an inert argon-gas shroud around the cut line.
- **Plasma Arc Tracking:** An internal stepper motor advances the plasma arc electrode along the pipe circumference at a controlled feed rate of 3.8 mm per second, burning through outer armor without severing the delicate interior copper busbars or glass optical ribbon cables.
- **Contact Spring Deployment:** Once penetration is complete, micro-actuated beryllium-copper spring fingers and optical prism taps snap down into the molten trench, establishing direct physical contact with 480V high-current power rails and terabit data lines.

### Inductive Siphon & Overdrive Power Surge
- **Massive Power Extraction:** The unit siphons up to 480 Volts and 14.2 Amperes of unmetered industrial current, routing it through high-efficiency solid-state buck converters to recharge cyberdeck capacitor banks or power external heavy combat cyberware.
- **Overdrive Surge Dynamics:** Forcing the unit into overdrive siphon draws power beyond conduit design limits. Under extreme extraction, the copper busbar superheats, triggering explosive electrical arcing and intense plasma spark showers that test the mechanical containment seals of the clamp.
