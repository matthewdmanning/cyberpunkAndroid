### Operational Interaction & Mechanics
- **The Actuator Core:** A tactile circular actuator embedded on the forearm or collarbone with an outer rotational reticle and a central capacitive contact pad.
- **Hold-to-Charge Capacitive Gate:**
  - The operator presses their thumb against the central contact pad and holds it down.
  - A radial sweep indicator fills clockwise around the perimeter reticle as bio-capacitors charge (1.2-second charge threshold).

- **State Transition:**
  - When the gate fully charges, the governor disengages with a sharp click and an audible high-frequency whine.
  - The indicator shifts from cool secure blue/cyan to hot uninhibited danger-red, confirming unfiltered reflex speed.
  - The central node displays an active electrical surge symbol.
- **Reversion:** Holding the contact pad again re-engages the safety dampeners, bleeding the surge back into ground lines.

## 5. Subdermal LN2 Cryo-Manifold // Cyberdeck Overclock Governor

### Canonical Context & Scenario
A closed-loop liquid nitrogen (LN2) subdermal cooling manifold coupled with an extreme-overclock cyberdeck processing core. Pushing a deck past its rated Thermal Design Power (TDP) to execute military-grade penetration daemons (e.g., Kuang Grade Eleven or Black ICE disruptors) generates massive thermal dissipation that threatens to cause thermal tissue damage in the runner's skull.

### Operational Interaction & Mechanics
- **The Physical Controls:**
  - A heavy mechanical toggle switch to engage the Overclock Bus multiplier (jumping the clock frequency from 3.2 GHz to 5.8 GHz).
  - A smooth proportional linear slider controlling the LN2 cryo-coolant injection pump (0% to 100% flow rate).
- **The Thermodynamic Balance Loop:**
  - Core temperature is governed by real-time thermodynamic balance: heat generated by the overclock minus heat dissipated by the coolant pump.
  - At idle, the manifold runs at body temperature (37°C) under nominal blue/cyan illumination.
  - Engaging the overclock without sufficient coolant causes the core temperature to spike past 85°C into red-line critical territory.
- **Dynamic Equipment Feedback:**
  - A circular thermal manifold gauge sweeps from cool cyan to amber warning and finally deep hot red.
  - A coolant turbine intake physically spins, with rotation velocity directly linked to the coolant slider position.
  - Above 85°C, thermal alarms fire, the display begins suffering severe electronic jitter and chromatic tearing, and safety vents dump excess pressure.
  - Raising the cryo-pump slider floods the core with liquid nitrogen, stabilizing temperatures back to safe operational limits.
